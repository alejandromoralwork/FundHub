{\large\bfseries Why managers come to FundHub}

\begin{multicols}{2}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Fees are hard to compare.} Proposals are bespoke, with hidden line items and out-of-pocket charges.
  \item \textbf{Recommendations aren't always neutral.} Providers are often introduced under referral fees.
  \item \textbf{Setting up means many decisions at once:} structure, AIFM or ManCo, depositary, administrator, auditor, domiciliation.

\end{itemize}
