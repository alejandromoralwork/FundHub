# Draft: request to LBR for a data extract (RCS)

**To:** Luxembourg Business Registers (LBR). Use the address for data reuse / helpdesk on lbr.lu.
**Subject:** Request for a re-use extract of the RCS register (list of registered entities)

Dear Sir or Madam,

FundHub (fundhub.lu) is a Luxembourg platform that helps investment funds compare and select their service providers: fund administrators, domiciliation agents, depositaries, ManCos and AIFMs. We want to base our company data on the official register rather than on secondary sources.

We would like to know under what conditions LBR can provide, under its data re-use terms, an extract of the Trade and Companies Register (RCS) that contains for each registered entity:

- the RCS number;
- the name, legal form and registered office;
- the registration date and current status (registered, in liquidation, struck off);
- ideally, the date of the latest filing.

We are also interested in the filings themselves for the companies in our scope (fund service providers, management companies and their funds, about a few thousand RCS numbers):

- the list of filings (number, date, type), and
- the filed documents (PDF), such as articles, annual accounts and lists of authorised signatories, with ongoing delivery of new filings.

We would also like to know:

1. the formats available (CSV, XML…) and how often updates or deltas can be delivered;
2. the licence terms, fees, and any restrictions on display and redistribution;
3. whether an official API or feed exists for daily changes (new registrations, filings).

We do not need any personal data about natural persons for this purpose. If directors or beneficial-owner data come within the scope of any extract, we will handle them under GDPR and LBR's conditions.

Thank you in advance. We are happy to discuss by phone or in a meeting.
